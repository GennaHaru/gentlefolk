Mixed Sessions

🎶 Brisbane Saturday Session 🎶

The Brisbane Saturday Acoustic Music Session is on at the Brunswick Hotel, 569 Brunswick St, New Farm every Saturday from 1.00pm-5.00pm.

This is a music jam session on acoustic instruments where we gather to have fun, celebrate music and good people and to just enjoy all of life.

We really are a bunch of friendly, fun people coming together to share music and having a laugh!!! It is a great place to share music, learn music, listen to music and have brilliant time while doing so.

All levels of ability are very welcome. We also love listeners coming along to sing along, clap or just enjoy. If you've been thinking about coming down, now is the time. Everyone is welcoming and very supportive....

When: 1pm-5pm Every Saturday
Where: The Brunswick Hotel, New Farm
Free

Tune Sessions

☘️ Brisbane Ceol Beginner Tunes ☘️

Join us for a welcoming, facilitated trad session dedicated to learning traditional Irish tunes and styles – perfect for beginner to intermediate musicians.

Where: IASAQ Office, 5 Abingdon St, Woolloongabba When: Every second Wednesday evening, 7:00pm – 9:00pm�🎻

Whether you're just starting out or building your Irish repertoire, Brisbane Ceol is a supported, friendly trad session teaching traditional music skills through Irish tunes.

Tuition may be available on traditional instruments including fiddle, tin whistle, mandolin, Irish concertina, DADGAD guitar and bodhrán.

No experience or music reading (though music notation is usually available) skill necessary – just bring your instrument and love of Celtic music!

Cost: Free for IASAQ Members. Membership is $10 a year and you can sign up on the night. Free parking on site at our office for the trad sessions.

When: 7pm-9pm every second Wednesday
Where: IASAQ Office, Woolloongabba
Free (with $10 annual membership)

🇮🇪🇸🇪 Good Tunes 🇮🇹🇫🇷

If you have an acoustic instrument and have an interest in playing a wide range of folk tunes from around the world then come along. All levels of musicians are welcome to join in.

There will be copies of the Good Tunes Book (Volumes 1 and 2 with over 300 tunes) to use. The tunes are written as single line melody and with guitar chords . You can also just use your ear if you wish to play along.

We'll play through jigs, reels, polkas and waltzes and more from all over the place including Ireland, Scotland, Italy, Spain, France, Quebec, Australia, England, USA, Scandinavia and beyond You can see a sample of the tunes at: http://www.goodtunes.com.au

VENUE: The Burrow - 52 Russell St, West End. QLD

About Genna's Gentlefolk

Genna's Gentlefolk is a project to share opportunities for everyone to participate in folk singing and playing.

Based in Magandjin (Brisbane, QLD)
On the Lands of the Turrbal and Jagera People

Performance and collaboration opportunities welcome.
